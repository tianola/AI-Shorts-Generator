# Ideas adapted from other projects

Clipify AI (https://github.com/sherlock-dev-ai/Clipify-AI-Video-Clipper-FREE), licensed
CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial). We read it for ideas and wrote our own implementation:

- 11-dimension clip judging with keep/reject and penalties: `services/clip_judging.py`
- editing modes, topic-aware clip windows: `services/clip_judging.py`, `services/clip_selector.py`
- gaming gameplay + facecam layout: `services/gaming_layout.py`
- logo / text branding: `services/branding.py`
- Narrate Studio (faceless shorts): `services/narrate.py`
- Live Studio (live highlight clipping): `services/live_studio.py`

This project must stay non-commercial while it carries these adaptations.
