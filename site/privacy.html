Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 October 2026

ClipForge is a desktop tool that runs on your machine. The short version: it processes your videos where they already are, and the only thing that ever leaves your computer is a clip you told it to upload, sent straight to the TikTok account you connected. This site itself sets no cookies and loads nothing from third parties.

Who is responsible

The developer of ClipForge, an individual based in Greece, is the controller of the very little personal data described here. The fastest contact is a DM to @ClippinEvrything; a postal address for formal requests is provided on request.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting, no usage tracking. There is no server for any of it to go to.
  • No cookies or local storage on this website, and no third-party scripts, fonts or images are loaded by these pages.
  • No access to your TikTok videos, followers, statistics, messages or wallet — the app requests only the profile-read and upload scopes it actually needs.
  • No data about your audience or about anyone who watches your clips.

Who it is shared with

Nobody, other than TikTok — and only the specific clip you chose to upload, sent by your own machine directly to TikTok's official Content Posting API under the authorisation you granted. There is no advertising network, no reseller, no processor in between.

How long it is kept

For as long as you keep it. Everything lives on your disk and is deleted when you delete it, or when you use the app's own prune/storage controls. Tokens are kept until you revoke them; transcripts and renders are kept until you remove them. Nothing is retained on any infrastructure I operate, because none of your material reaches any.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability or to object to processing. Because the data sits on your own machine, in almost every case the faster route is deleting the file or folder — but if you want to exercise a right formally, send the request through the contact above and it will be handled within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Hellenic Data Protection Authority.

Children

This tool is for adults managing their own content and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date at the top changes and the app shows a note on next start.
