1. What this is

ClipForge is a video editing tool that runs on your own computer. It takes a video you supply, cuts it into short vertical clips and writes the results back to your disk. Where you choose to connect a TikTok account, it uploads finished clips to that account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

2. Your side of the deal

  • You only point it at material you have the right to edit and republish — your own recordings, or content licensed to you.
  • You are responsible for what ends up on your TikTok account. Clips land in your inbox precisely so that you, not the tool, decide what gets posted.
  • You use it for one account: the one you authorise. No bulk accounts, no posting on other people's behalf without their own consent.
  • You do not use it to spam, to repost other creators' work as yours, or to evade a platform's rules.

3. What I promise

I will not take actions on your TikTok account beyond uploading a clip for your review, unless you explicitly change that setting yourself. I will not sell your data, because there is no pipeline that carries it anywhere except your own machine and TikTok.
